The student experience

From setup to review

Students start with their own notes or PDFs, then choose multiple-choice, true/false, or written-response questions. Each path leads from practice to feedback and review.

01 / Create a quiz

From course notes
to practice questions

Start with a PDF, pasted notes, or both. Choose a question format, and the app generates a ten-question practice quiz in the background.

02 / Check understanding

Multiple choice
& true/false

A quick check of understanding: answer, get an explanation, then review the results. Multiple-choice and true/false quizzes follow the same flow.

03 / Explain in your own words

Written responses
& AI feedback

Written-response mode asks students to explain a concept in their own words. Each answer is graded in the background against the question and a model answer.

Engineering

Built around
background work.

Generation and written-response grading run as background jobs. FastAPI enqueues work through RabbitMQ, the message broker. Separate Dramatiq workers process those jobs and call Azure OpenAI.

Validated before storage

The generation worker parses model output as JSON and validates its structure with Pydantic before saving questions in PostgreSQL. Sessions, responses, and job state are also stored in the database.

Updates without a refresh

The client watches for WebSocket job updates and polls for questions every three seconds. When generation completes, it fetches the questions and stops polling.

Results without a round trip

The browser calculates multiple-choice and true/false scores immediately; a background request asks the backend to calculate and save the final score. Written-response summaries are calculated in the browser from the individual AI grading results.
